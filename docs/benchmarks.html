Benchmarks

Frozen embeddings and one linear probe for every model. ssmix is never trained; the probe is the only fitted component.

Protocol

  • Probe: StandardScaler, then multinomial logistic regression. C is chosen from {10−3, 10−2, 10−1, 1} by 3-fold cross-validation inside each training split. The probe is identical for every model.
  • ESC-50: 2,000 five-second clips in 50 environmental classes, official 5-fold cross-validation.
  • FSDD: Free Spoken Digit Dataset, 3,000 recordings of the digits 0–9, official split (takes 0–4 form the test set). Clips are zero-padded to 2.3 s; PANNs CNN14 receives each clip at its true length.
  • The ssmix configuration was selected on ESC-50 folds 1–4 only; fold 5 never influenced a design choice.
  • Pretrained baselines use the authors' released weights; wav2vec 2.0 uses the mean-pooled states of all 13 layers.

  • At 16,384 tokens the cross-scale mixer is 4.3× faster than parameter-free softmax attention (351.1 ms vs 1,495.8 ms). Its cost grows near-linearly in T, while attention grows quadratically.
  • The tight frame's cost is flat in support: 43.8 ms with db1, 42.8 ms with db4 and 50.3 ms with db16 at 16,384 tokens.
  • Embedding all 2,000 ESC-50 clips (10,000 s of audio) takes 197 s on the same CPU.
