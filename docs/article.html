ssmix: Training-Free Scale-Space Transformers for Audio on Daubechies Wavelets

Angshul Majumdar · Indraprastha Institute of Information Technology Delhi

Abstract. We present ssmix, an audio transformer with no learnable parameters. Its token mixer acts on an exact multiscale representation: an undecimated Daubechies frame whose filter support is a free parameter. All scales are computed in one batched FFT; the frame is Parseval-tight, so reconstruction is exact. On this frame, every coefficient attends to its dyadic siblings, parent and children through parameter-free cosine attention. The resulting embedder needs no data, no pretraining and no fine-tuning. With a linear probe on frozen embeddings, it reaches 74.6% on ESC-50. That is ahead of VGGish (71.0%), wav2vec 2.0 (68.4%), openSMILE ComParE (71.2%) and the concatenation of eight established hand-crafted feature sets (73.9%). On spoken digits (FSDD) it reaches 99.0%, ahead of PaSST (98.0%), EfficientAT (98.7%), wav2vec 2.0 (97.0%) and PANNs (85.7%). Because nothing is learned, ssmix is intended for low-resource settings, where pretrained models have no matching data.

1. Introduction

Transformers learn how tokens mix. That learning costs data, compute and, for audio, large curated corpora such as AudioSet or thousands of hours of speech. Many settings have none of these: under-documented languages, specialised acoustic domains, small institutional collections. Hand-crafted features need no data, but they are fixed summaries; they do not mix information across time and scale the way an attention layer does.

ssmix keeps the transformer's structure (tokens, a token mixer, a readout) but replaces every learned component with an exact operator. Wavelets are the natural basis: they separate a signal into scales with compact, controllable support, and orthonormal Daubechies families give perfect reconstruction. The mixer is attention over the wavelet tree: each coefficient looks at its neighbours in scale and time.

Contributions.

  1. A GPU-batched Daubechies tight frame for any order and any sequence length. All scales come from one FFT, the cost does not depend on filter support, and reconstruction is exact.
  2. A parameter-free cross-scale attention mixer on that frame. It is shift-equivariant, 1-homogeneous, exactly the identity in its zero-temperature limit, and linear in sequence length.
  3. A training-free audio embedder whose readout includes the attention maps themselves. Under an identical linear probe it is ahead of VGGish, wav2vec 2.0, openSMILE ComParE and eight established hand-crafted feature sets on ESC-50.

2. Related work

The wavelet scattering transform [1, 2] is the classic training-free deep network: cascades of wavelet transforms and modulus non-linearities. ssmix differs by mixing coefficients through data-dependent attention across scales, rather than by fixed cascades. FNet [3] and WaveMix [4] replace attention with Fourier and wavelet operators, but they keep learned layers and are trained end to end. Hand-crafted audio descriptors, from MFCCs to the openSMILE eGeMAPS [5] and ComParE [6] sets, remain the standard training-free baselines. Pretrained embedders, among them VGGish [7], wav2vec 2.0 [8], PANNs [9], PaSST [10] and EfficientAT [11], are the standard learned ones.

3. Method

3.1 Daubechies filters of any order

Order \(N\) gives \(N\) vanishing moments and support \(2N-1\) [12]. The lowpass filter is obtained from \(|H(\omega)|^2 = 2\cos^{2N}(\omega/2)\,P(\sin^2(\omega/2))\), with \(P(y)=\sum_{k\lt N}\binom{N-1+k}{k}y^k\), by spectral factorisation. The roots of \(P\) are found at high precision, and the minimum-phase factor is kept. The highpass is \(g[n]=(-1)^n h[L-1-n]\). The filters are identical to PyWavelets for db1–db38 (max deviation below \(10^{-12}\)). Orthonormality and vanishing moments are verified to db60.

3.2 A tight frame in one FFT

For the undecimated transform of a length-\(T\) sequence, the band transfer functions are

\[ D_j(\omega)=\tilde G(2^{j-1}\omega)\prod_{k\lt j-1}\tilde H(2^k\omega),\qquad A_J(\omega)=\prod_{k\lt J}\tilde H(2^k\omega),\qquad \tilde H=H/\sqrt2,\ \tilde G=G/\sqrt2. \]

Since \(|\tilde H|^2+|\tilde G|^2=1\), the sum telescopes to \(\sum_s|\Psi_s(\omega)|^2=1\), a Parseval tight frame; the adjoint is the exact inverse for every \(T\). Evaluated on the DFT grid, all \(J+1\) bands come from one forward FFT and one batched inverse, independent of the filter support. Each band is shifted by its integer group delay (a pure phase), so index \(t\) denotes the same instant at every scale. Translation-invariant analysis of this kind goes back to [13, 14].

3.3 Cross-scale attention

Tokens are the coefficients \(w_s(t)\in\mathbb R^C\). Token \((s,t)\) attends to

\[ \mathcal N(s,t)=\{(s',\,t+o\,2^{\ell(s')}) : s'\in\{s-1,s,s+1\},\ |o|\le k\}, \]

its siblings, parent and children on the undecimated dyadic grid. With per-head cosine scores,

\[ a_n=\operatorname{softmax}_{n\in\mathcal N(s,t)}\frac{\langle\hat w_s(t),\hat w_n\rangle}{\tau},\qquad w'_s(t)=\sum_{n\in\mathcal N(s,t)}a_n\,w_n , \]

followed by exact synthesis. The operator has no parameters. It is shift-equivariant and 1-homogeneous. As \(\tau\to0\) it is exactly the identity, because a token's cosine with itself is maximal. Memory is \(O(SCT)\) and work \(O(SCT(2k+1)\cdot3)\), linear in \(T\). Each head's channels are kept contiguous, and keys are never materialised.

3.4 The embedder

Audio at 32 kHz becomes a 128-band log-mel sequence (25 ms windows, 10 ms hop), normalised per clip. Two blocks of cross-scale attention follow (db4, 16 heads, \(k=2\), \(\tau=0.1\)). For each block, the readout pools over time:

  • band magnitudes before and after mixing;
  • the mean attention weight of every cross-scale link and head, a direct measurement of how each scale couples to its parent, children and siblings;
  • a spectral Daubechies scale space along the mel axis, and the state statistics.

The mean log-mel envelope is appended. The configuration was chosen on ESC-50 folds 1–4 and spoken-digit development speakers only.

4. Experiments

4.1 Protocol

Every model produces a frozen clip embedding. One probe is used throughout: standardisation followed by multinomial logistic regression, with C chosen from \(\{10^{-3},10^{-2},10^{-1},1\}\) by 3-fold cross-validation inside each training split.

  • ESC-50 [15]: 2,000 five-second clips in 50 classes, official 5-fold cross-validation.
  • FSDD [16]: 3,000 spoken digits, official split.

Pretrained models use their released weights. wav2vec 2.0 is given the mean-pooled states of all 13 layers, the strongest standard frozen readout under a linear probe. PANNs receives each digit at its true length.

4.4 Exactness and cost

The frame reconstructs to \(10^{-12}\) in float64 for every tested length, prime lengths included. The decimated transform matches PyWavelets' periodized DWT to machine precision. At 16,384 tokens the cross-scale mixer runs 4.3× faster than parameter-free softmax attention on CPU, and the frame's cost is flat in filter support (43.8 ms for db1, 50.3 ms for db16). Embedding all of ESC-50 (10,000 s of audio) takes 197 s on two CPU threads.

5. Conclusion

A transformer's token mixer does not have to be learned. Exact wavelet analysis with controllable support, together with attention defined on the wavelet tree, gives an audio embedder with zero parameters. Under an identical linear probe, it is ahead of VGGish, wav2vec 2.0 and the established hand-crafted feature sets on environmental sound. It needs no data at all, which makes it a natural starting point for low-resource corpora.
