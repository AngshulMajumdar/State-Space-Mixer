Method

Three exact building blocks: Daubechies filters of controllable support, a tight frame that computes every scale in one batched FFT, and a parameter-free cross-scale attention defined on that frame.

1. Daubechies filters of any order

Order \(N\) gives \(N\) vanishing moments and support \(2N-1\). The lowpass filter is obtained from \(|H(\omega)|^2 = 2\cos^{2N}(\omega/2)\,P(\sin^2(\omega/2))\) with \(P(y)=\sum_{k\lt N}\binom{N-1+k}{k}y^k\). Each root of \(P\) maps to a pair \(z, 1/z\); keeping \(|z|\lt 1\) gives the extremal-phase factor. Roots are found at high precision with mpmath, so large orders remain exact. The highpass is \(g[n] = (-1)^n h[L-1-n]\).

2. A tight frame in one FFT

The undecimated transform of a length-\(T\) signal has the band transfer functions

\[ D_j(\omega)=\tilde G(2^{j-1}\omega)\prod_{k\lt j-1}\tilde H(2^k\omega),\qquad A_J(\omega)=\prod_{k\lt J}\tilde H(2^k\omega),\qquad \tilde H = H/\sqrt2,\ \tilde G = G/\sqrt2 . \]

Because \(|\tilde H|^2+|\tilde G|^2=1\), the sum telescopes to \(\sum_s|\Psi_s(\omega)|^2=1\): a Parseval tight frame, with exact reconstruction by the adjoint for any \(T\). The responses are evaluated on the DFT grid, so all \(J+1\) bands come from one rFFT and one batched inverse. The cost, \(O(T\log T)\) per band, does not depend on the filter support. Each band receives an integer group-delay compensation (a pure phase), so index \(t\) refers to the same instant at every scale.

3. Cross-scale attention

Tokens are the frame coefficients \(w_s(t)\in\mathbb R^C\). Token \((s,t)\) attends to the neighbourhood

\[ \mathcal N(s,t)=\{(s',\,t+o\,2^{\ell(s')}) : s'\in\{s-1,s,s+1\},\ |o|\le k\}, \]

that is, its dyadic siblings, parent and children on the undecimated grid (\(\ell\) is the dyadic level of a band). With per-head cosine weights

\[ a_{n}=\operatorname{softmax}_{n\in\mathcal N(s,t)}\frac{\langle\hat w_s(t),\hat w_n\rangle}{\tau},\qquad w'_s(t)=\sum_{n\in\mathcal N(s,t)}a_n\,w_n , \]

followed by the exact synthesis. The operator has no parameters. It is shift-equivariant and 1-homogeneous, and it is exactly the identity as \(\tau\to0\), because each token's cosine with itself is maximal. Each head's channels are contiguous and keys are never materialised. Memory is therefore \(O(SCT)\) and the work is \(O(SCT\,(2k+1)\cdot 3)\), linear in sequence length.

4. Audio embedder

The AudioEmbedder runs at 32 kHz with 128 mel bands (25 ms window, 10 ms hop) and per-clip normalisation, followed by two cross-scale mixer blocks (db4, 16 heads, \(k=2\), \(\tau=0.1\)). For each block the readout pools over time:

  • band magnitudes \(\log\overline{|w_s|}\) before and after mixing;
  • the mean attention weight of every cross-scale link and head, which records how strongly each scale couples to its parent, children and siblings;
  • a spectral scale space along the mel axis, and the state statistics.

The mean log-mel envelope is appended once, at the end.
